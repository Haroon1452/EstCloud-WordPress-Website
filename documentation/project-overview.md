# EstCloud OU – Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

EstCloud OU is an Estonia-based Managed Service Provider (MSP) and IT consulting company helping European organizations modernize, secure, and optimize their IT environments.

The company provides customized cloud computing, cybersecurity, and IT consulting services designed to support business continuity, operational efficiency, and digital transformation.

**Website:** https://estcloud.ee/

## 2. Project Objectives

The objective of the website project is to establish a professional digital presence for EstCloud OU and communicate its technology services to prospective business customers.

The website is designed to:

- Present EstCloud's cloud and IT consulting services.
- Explain managed IT and cybersecurity solutions.
- Establish a professional corporate identity.
- Help businesses understand available service offerings.
- Encourage potential customers to request consultations.
- Provide clear navigation across company information and services.

## 3. Business Overview

EstCloud focuses on helping organizations manage their technology infrastructure through customized and security-oriented services.

### Core Business Areas

- Managed IT Services
- Microsoft 365 Administration
- Microsoft Azure Infrastructure
- Cloud Strategy and Consulting
- Cybersecurity and Compliance
- IT Infrastructure Management
- Cloud Migration and Optimization
- Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery
